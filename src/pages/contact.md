# Contact

Omarchy Themes is run by [Limehawk](https://limehawk.io) in Knoxville, Tennessee. Pick the channel that fits what you need, and we will answer as soon as we can.

## Email

[help@omarchytheme.com](mailto:help@omarchytheme.com) reaches the people who run the site. Use it for anything private: a takedown request, a security problem in a listed theme, or a question about your data.

## Submit or fix a theme

- **Add your theme:** [open a submission](https://github.com/limehawk/omarchy-theme-website/issues/new?template=submit-theme.yml) with your repository URL.
- **Fix a listing:** if a name, preview, or palette is wrong, [open an issue](https://github.com/limehawk/omarchy-theme-website/issues) with the theme's URL on this site.
- **Remove your theme:** email us or open an issue from the GitHub account that owns the repository.

## Request a theme

Want a theme that does not exist yet? Ask in [theme requests](https://github.com/limehawk/omarchy-theme-website/discussions/categories/theme-requests). Theme authors read that list.

## Omarchy itself

We cannot help with installing or fixing Omarchy. For that, see [omarchy.org](https://omarchy.org/) and the Omarchy community.
