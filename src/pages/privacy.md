# Privacy

Omarchy Themes has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, and no tracking cookies. This page lists everything that happens to your data when you visit.

## What we collect

- **Page views.** We use [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/) to count visits. It does not set cookies or use local storage, and it does not build a profile of you. We see totals such as page views, referring sites, and countries.
- **Server logs.** [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) hosts the site. Like any host, it processes your IP address and browser details to deliver pages and stop attacks.
- **Email.** If you write to help@omarchytheme.com, we keep your message so we can answer it. We do not add you to a mailing list.

## Other services your browser contacts

- **Google Fonts.** Pages load the JetBrains Mono and Workbench fonts from Google, so Google receives your IP address. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **GitHub.** Theme READMEs show images from the theme's GitHub repository, so GitHub receives your IP address when you open a theme page. Links to GitHub take you to GitHub, under [its privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## What we do not do

We do not sell or share data, run ad or social trackers, or store anything in your browser. The browse page keeps your filters in the URL, not in storage.

## Questions

Email [help@omarchytheme.com](mailto:help@omarchytheme.com). We will update this page if any of this changes.
